Did you know? The square of a negative number is always positive: (-4)² = 16.
Square root (√) gives the non-negative root. For example √16 = 4 (and also -4 is a root, but principal root is 4).
Perfect squares have integer square roots: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ...
